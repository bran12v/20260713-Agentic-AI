# Records Retention Schedule

**Doc id:** RET-SCH
**Doc type:** schedule
**Owner:** Information Governance
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Application

This schedule sets the minimum retention period for records generated by Delivery
Engineering. Minimum means minimum: a record may be held longer where a legal hold
applies, and a legal hold overrides every period below.

Retention runs from the record's closure date unless stated otherwise. For change
records, closure means the change reached a terminal state — implemented, backed
out, or withdrawn — not the date it was submitted.

## 2. Schedule

| Record class | Minimum retention | Runs from |
|---|---|---|
| Change records, normal | 3 years | Change closure |
| Change records, emergency, with retrospective review | 7 years | Review closure |
| CAB minutes and decisions | 7 years | Session date |
| Standard Change Catalog revisions | 7 years | Supersession |
| Incident records, Severity 1 and 2 | 7 years | Incident closure |
| Incident records, Severity 3 and 4 | 2 years | Incident closure |
| Post-incident review records | 7 years | Review publication |
| Production access grants and approvals | 3 years | Grant expiry |
| Production session logs, Elevated and break-glass | 3 years | Session end |
| Production session logs, Read and Operate | 1 year | Session end |
| Vendor security review records | 6 years | Vendor relationship end |
| Freeze exceptions and standing control exceptions | 7 years | Expiry |

## 3. Deletion

Records reaching the end of their retention period are queued for deletion by the
automated lifecycle job, which runs nightly.

**A queued record is held for a 24-hour grace period before deletion.** During the
grace period the record remains readable and any owner may cancel the deletion by
applying a hold. After the grace period the deletion is irreversible and is itself
recorded in the deletion register.

The grace period exists for one reason: retention periods are calculated from
dates that are sometimes corrected after the fact. A change record whose closure
date was recorded wrongly can reach its deletion date early, and the grace period
is the window in which that is caught.

## 4. The deletion register

Every deletion is recorded with the record class, the identifier, the retention
period applied, the calculated expiry, and the date of deletion. The register is
itself retained for 10 years and is never subject to the lifecycle job.

## 5. Legal hold

A legal hold suspends deletion for the records in its scope, including records
already in the 24-hour grace period. A hold is applied and released only by
Information Governance, and both actions are recorded in the register.

Records under hold are excluded from the nightly job entirely — they are not
queued and then cancelled, they are never queued.

## 6. Responsibility

Record owners are responsible for correct classification at creation.
Misclassification that results in premature deletion is a control failure, and the
deletion register is the evidence used to establish it.
